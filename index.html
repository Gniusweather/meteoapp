SPECI vs METAR — Quick Rule
METARRoutine — issued every 30 min at :00 and :30 UTC
SPECIImmediate — issued as soon as any criterion above is triggered. Not time-bound. Next routine METAR cancels the SPECI status
TrendAfter a SPECI, continue to monitor — issue additional SPECIs if further threshold crossings occur

TAF AMD vs COR — Quick Rule
TAFRoutine forecast — issued 1–2 h before validity start. Valid 24 h or 30 h
TAF AMDAmendment — issued immediately when conditions differ from forecast by crossing a criterion above and are expected to persist. Replaces the running TAF from that time onward
TAF CORCorrection — issued to fix an error in an already-issued TAF (typo, wrong value). The original time is retained
NILTAF NIL — confirms no TAF will be issued for the period

Change Groups
FMFrom — permanent change from an exact time (FMDDHHmm). Replaces all elements of the base forecast from that point. No end time
BECMGBecoming — gradual change expected to occur within the specified period (DDHH/DDHH). Change completes by the end time and persists
TEMPOTemporary — fluctuating conditions each lasting < 60 min, totalling < half the period. Reverts to base forecast between occurrences
PROB3030% probability of conditions during the period. Cannot precede TEMPO or appear in first 6 h
PROB4040% probability. Can be used with TEMPO: PROB40 TEMPO
CAVOK Conditions (all three must be met)
1Visibility ≥ 10 km (9999 or no restriction)
2No cloud below 1 500 m (5 000 ft) and no Cb at any level
3No significant weather (no precipitation, fog, dust, squall, etc.)
Validity Periods & Issue Times (ICAO Annex 3 §5.1.3)
Standard24 h validity; issue 1–2 h before start of validity
Extended30 h validity where operationally required
AMD timingIssued as soon as the forecaster determines the criterion is met and expected to persist — no delay
CancellationTAF CNL DDHHmmZ DDHH/DDHH — cancels a TAF before its validity starts
Weather Descriptors & Phenomena
TSThunderstorm — always encode as TS + phenomenon (TSRA, TSGR) or TS alone if no precipitation
SHShower — SHRA, SHSN, SHGR; implies Cb or deep Cu; intermittent by nature
FZFreezing — FZRA, FZDZ, FZFG; always critical for aircraft icing
BL / DRBlowing / Low drifting: BLSN, BLDU, BLSA / DRBLSN. BLSN reduces vis; DR stays below 2 m
